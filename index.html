Remedminder Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Remedminder handles information when you use the Remedminder medication reminder application. Please read this policy before using the application.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Remedminder may process the following information:

  • Account information: Information associated with your account, such as your email address and authentication details.
  • Medication and reminder information: Medication names, schedules, dosage details you enter, and reminder settings.
  • Profile and community information: Profile details, posts, or other information you choose to provide through profile or community features.
  • AI feature inputs: Questions, prompts, and other information you submit when using AI-powered features.
  • Technical information: Information that may be processed by the application or its service providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save and synchronize information across supported devices or services.
  • Schedule medication reminders and display notifications.
  • Provide profile, community, reporting, and AI-powered features.
  • Maintain application security, troubleshoot problems, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to privacy requests and user inquiries.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

Remedminder uses local device storage for certain application data and may use Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support account authentication and online data storage or synchronization.

If you use AI-powered features, information you submit may be processed by the AI service configured for that feature. Information handled by third-party services is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

The specific information processed depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the application.

4. Medication Information and Notifications

Medication names, schedules, and related details you enter may be stored locally or synchronized through supported online services. Reminder notifications may display medication-related information on your device, depending on your settings and operating system.

Please consider your device's notification privacy settings if you do not want medication information to appear on your lock screen.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support application functionality, or shared when required by applicable law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Information you choose to post through community or sharing features may be visible to other users or recipients, depending on the feature and its settings. Do not post sensitive information publicly.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's features, maintain security, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period.

To request account deletion or deletion of personal information, contact us at the email address below. Please identify your request clearly. We may need to verify your identity before processing it. Deletion of an account or online data may not automatically remove data already stored on every device or in backups.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Remedminder is not intended to collect personal information from children without the required consent or authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Health Disclaimer

Remedminder is a medication reminder and information tool. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow instructions from your healthcare professional. Do not rely solely on this application for time-critical medication needs or emergencies.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
